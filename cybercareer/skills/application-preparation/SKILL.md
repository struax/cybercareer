# Skill: Application preparation

## Objective
Prepare accurate answers and application materials for human review.

## Rules
- Populate fields only from confirmed candidate information.
- If an answer is unknown, leave it blank or ask the candidate.
- Never guess salary history, eligibility, work authorization, clearance, demographic information, or legal attestations.
- Never accept legal terms or certify statements on the candidate's behalf.
- Never click a final submit button.

## Deliverables
- Draft answers organized by application field.
- Any required information that is missing.
- A final review checklist.
